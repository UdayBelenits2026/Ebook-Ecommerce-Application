1. Introduction

Welcome to Ebook. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide while using our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you use our website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, purchase a book, contact us, submit a review, or use other features of our website.

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Billing and shipping address
  • Account information
  • Order and purchase information
  • Reviews and other information you submit

Technical Information

We may also collect technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, pages visited and general usage information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing your account.
  • Processing and delivering orders.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Processing payments.
  • Managing your wishlist and shopping cart.
  • Displaying and managing reviews.
  • Improving our website and services.
  • Communicating with you about your account or orders.
  • Detecting and preventing fraudulent or unauthorized activity.
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements.

4. Account Information

If you create an account on Ebook, you are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.

Please notify us if you believe that your account has been accessed without authorization.

5. Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. Payment information may be handled directly by those providers according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

We do not intentionally store sensitive payment credentials such as complete card security information on our servers unless specifically required for the payment service we use.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, improve functionality, and understand how visitors use our website.

You can configure your browser to restrict or disable cookies. However, disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

7. Reviews and User Content

If you submit a review or other public content, information associated with that submission may be displayed to other website visitors.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in reviews or other publicly visible content.

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information for monetary gain.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate our website and provide services, including payment processing, hosting, delivery and communication services.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect our rights, users, systems or property.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain information.
  • Request information about how your data is used.
  • Withdraw certain permissions where applicable.

12. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use online services without parental or guardian involvement.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us so that we can review and address the situation.
